# Visual system
- Read app_spacing, app_radius, app_typography, app_motion, app_tokens and theme_extensions before proposing tokens.
- Reuse FptButton/FptCard/FptTextField contracts; evolve wrappers incrementally.
- Use semantic colors in both themes; preserve context.fpt consumers during a bridge.
- Propose a coherent direction and one representative screen before sweeping redesign.
- Vietnamese academic content, AI conversation readability and evidence/source clarity come before decorative effects.
- Avoid per-screen token forks, stock web-dashboard layouts, arbitrary gradients and dense desktop controls on phones.
- Design loading, empty, partial, error, disabled and success states, not only populated mockups.
